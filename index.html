Seismic Waves (P & S)

Earthquakes release energy in seismic waves. Primary (P) waves are compressional and travel fastest through rock and liquid. Secondary (S) waves are shear waves moving side-to-side, causing severe structural shaking.

Earthquake Engineering

Modern structures utilize base isolation pads, tuned mass dampers, and ductile steel frames to absorb seismic energy, decoupling ground motion from the building superstructure.

Preparedness & Safety

During shaking: Drop, Cover, and Hold On under sturdy furniture. Stay away from glass windows and exterior walls. If outdoors, move to an open area away from buildings and power lines.

Step 2: Upload to GitHub & Enable Pages

  1. Go to GitHub.com and create a new public repository (e.g., earthquake-simulator).
  2. Upload your downloaded index.html file to the main branch.
  3. Go to your repository Settings > Pages.
  4. Under Build and deployment, set the branch to main / (root) and click Save. Your app will be live instantly!
